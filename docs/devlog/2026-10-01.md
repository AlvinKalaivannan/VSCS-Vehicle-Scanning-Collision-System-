**What failed / surprised me:**

- **Tracking: one outlier spawned a duplicate track.** A 99% gate rejects ~1% of genuine
  detections, and each miss started a duplicate. Fixed with a recovery stage (99.99%
  gate) before a new track is born.
- **Perception: side-clipped detector boxes bias position.** A walker cut off at the
  image edge read −0.69 m/s instead of −1.0. Top-clipped boxes are harmless.
- **CORRECTION.** I first blamed an early "critical" alert in the end-to-end drive on the
  fattened pole (box perception). With mask-accurate perception the alert is unchanged.
  The real cause is near-miss grading:
  - A near miss is graded by its closest approach over the whole horizon (ADR 0005).
  - So a 0.30 m bumper pass 2.2 s away (0.215 m after margins) grades critical *now*.
  - Proposed fix: ADR 0010. It needs a schema field (`t_closest_s`): the developer's
    decision.

  The docs, the RISKS row and the tests were all corrected.
