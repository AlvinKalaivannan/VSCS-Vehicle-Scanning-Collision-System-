**What failed / surprised me:**

- **The marker investigation, which took several wrong turns before the right answer.**
  Rendering markers into the fixture views gave corner errors up to 6 px.
  1. Anti-aliasing the renderer (4x supersample) cut the median but left a 3-4 px tail.
  2. I then gated on mean marker size, having wrongly concluded size was the cause. It did not
     fix it; neither did shortest side.
  3. The cause is **grazing viewing angle**: a flat ground marker seen from a standing
     walk-round is a thin trapezoid whose acute corners are poorly located along their edges.
     The smallest interior angle predicted the error best (r = -0.63). A 30 deg gate keeps
     errors near or under 1 px.
  4. With that gate, most flat markers have fewer than two usable views, so they cannot be
     triangulated. That is a **scan-day finding**, not a test artefact.
  5. Boards propped at 45 deg looked far better at first (0.33 px) - but my renderer had no
     back-face culling and printed the boards' face inward, so they were being "seen" through
     the van from behind. Fixed. Corrected result: 3-4 gated views at every board position,
     worst corner 0.5-1.2 px. Still clearly better than flat markers; less dramatic than the
     artefact suggested.
  Lesson: when a result improves dramatically, check the measurement before believing it.
- **A contract error of my own, caught before hand-over.** I first defined the marker spread
  as median absolute deviation, which is exactly zero whenever two of three markers agree -
  so a 10% misprint on the third would never be flagged. Changed to (max - min) / median;
  the estimate stays the median.
- **A control character slipped into CLAUDE.md.** A `\a` in a Python string became a BEL
  byte while syncing §12. Caught by reading the diff, fixed, and a scan confirms no tracked
  file has control characters.
- **COLMAP dense needs CUDA.** The laptop's no-CUDA COLMAP can do SfM but not
  `patch_match_stereo`, so P1-T7's planned dense primary cannot run here. Recorded in the
  ADR 0003 addendum as an open choice for the developer.
- The fixture camera (1280 px, f = 900) is a low-resolution stand-in. A real 150 mm marker at
  4K (f ~ 2740 px) looks like a 0.457 m marker to it, which is what the tests use.
