4. Recording and hub marks, every pass

  • 1080p60 · focus and exposure locked · IMU logger running · shake or clap at the start and end of each recording
  • Doors and mirrors: all closed (say so) · stop the van before touching the phone
  • Before moving and after stopping: plumb from the centres of the rear-left, rear-right and front-left hubs; chalk; tape x and y
  • Read each tape twice · left = the van's own left (driver's seat, facing forward)
  • Rear marks should be one rear track apart (±3 cm); if not, measure again

5. Pass plan (aim for 12–15)

Straight toward the pole · straight toward the boxes · left curve past an obstacle · right curve past an obstacle · parallel to the kerb · a pass where the mirror is nearest · a pass where a wheel is nearest · one or two very slow and one or two a little faster (< 5 km/h) · one control pass with nothing nearby.

6. After the passes, and the same day

  • Re-measure two obstacles; record any that moved and which passes they affect
  • Play back two clips
  • Copy (not move) → scripts/ingest.py --kind lot → three copies, each verified after copying
  • Type up gt.yaml (ADR 0012 format, including wheelbase_m) into the raw folder
  • Devlog today: dimensions, marker layout, mount, doors, surprises
